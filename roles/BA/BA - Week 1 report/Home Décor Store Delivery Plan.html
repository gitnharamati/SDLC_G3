Home Décor Store: 8-Week Delivery Schedule

Four increments plus an inception week, covering all 47 requirements from User Story 1 (storefront) and User Story 2 (admin back office). Each increment runs analysis → design → build → test → demo, with the BA working one increment ahead of development and QA testing in the same increment as it is built.

How the increments are cut

  • Admin first. The storefront needs real catalog data, so Increment 1 delivers sign-in, the hierarchy and products with per-combination stock (RQ3, RQ4).
  • Every increment is usable. Inc 1 ends with an admin who can load the catalog; Inc 2 with a shopper who can browse and fill a cart; Inc 3 with an end-to-end test order.
  • All Must-haves land by week 7. Week 8 holds the Should-haves, UAT and release, so slippage there costs only optional scope.
  • NFR-01 is tested every increment with a phone/desktop smoke pass, then fully in week 8.

Risks and assumptions

  • Developer capacity is the constraint. 47 hours across 47 requirements leaves about one hour each. Inc 3 (transactions, concurrency, status engine, contesting) is the heaviest; any overrun pushes Should-haves out.
  • CUS-16–18 (multi-add) is a stretch goal. If week 8 is tight, ship the minimum version (button disabled until all options chosen) or defer to Release 2.
  • Email/SMS (CUS-24) assumes a sandbox provider account is ready by week 6; the BA confirms this in week 4.
  • Order contesting (ADM-20) needs its workflow agreed by week 5, since no detail exists yet beyond RQ2.
  • Hours are focused effort; meetings beyond the listed demos come out of the same budget.
